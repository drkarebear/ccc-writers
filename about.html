About the project

Make the writing community visible.

CCC Writers is a working-title project for an independent statewide resource connecting California community college creative writers with literary journals, submission opportunities, events, and university pathways.

Design principles

  • Student questions come before institutional organization.
  • The map is never the only way to access journal information.
  • Dates and eligibility are marked with verification dates.
  • Unknown information is labeled unknown rather than guessed.
  • Community updates are moderated before publication.
  • The site aims for WCAG 2.2 AA and keyboard-friendly navigation.

Not an official CCCCO site

This project is independent and does not represent the California Community Colleges Chancellor’s Office, a college district, CSU, or UC.

Data philosophy

Prefer official journal and university sources. Keep a “last verified” date. Link users back to the original source before they submit work or make transfer decisions.

Accessibility

CCC Writers aims to meet WCAG 2.2 AA and to provide equivalent ways to reach information when an interactive feature, such as a map, is not the best format for a visitor.

If something on the site creates an accessibility barrier, report an accessibility issue by email. The email link can include the page address automatically. If you are comfortable doing so, describe what you were trying to do and the barrier you encountered; browser, device, or assistive technology information is optional.
