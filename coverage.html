Journal Research Coverage

The goal is to check every California community college without turning incomplete evidence into a false yes or no. The census now separates verified active journals, confirmed publication efforts that still need details, recent leads with unclear current status, and colleges that remain under research.

What the Labels Mean

Verified active journal

A current or sufficiently recent authoritative source identifies an active, named literary publication connected to the college.

Active publication — details needed

Current evidence says a literary publication effort exists, but a title, current issue, submission page, or another key detail still needs direct verification.

Recent/historical lead — current status unclear

A named publication is documented, but the latest available evidence is not strong enough to call it active in 2026.

Still researching

The search is incomplete or has not produced a strong enough lead. This never means that the college has no literary journal.

No active journal located

This label appears only after a deliberate review of current English, creative writing, arts, student publication, catalog, and site-search pages does not locate an active journal.

Historical/inactive journal located

A past journal is documented and evidence indicates that it stopped publishing or is explicitly inactive.

System count is based on the California Community Colleges Chancellor’s Office current system description. The census excludes the two continuing education institutions that appear in the Chancellor’s Office alphabetical directory but are not part of the stated 116-college count.
