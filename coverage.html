What the Labels Mean

Still researching ≠ no journal

This means the evidence is still incomplete or ambiguous. It keeps a partial search, an unverified lead, or a possible revival from becoming a false claim.

No active journal located

This label should appear only after a deliberate search of the college’s current English, creative writing, arts, student publication, catalog, and site search pages fails to locate an active journal.

System count is based on the California Community Colleges Chancellor’s Office current system description. The census excludes the two continuing education institutions that appear in the Chancellor’s Office alphabetical directory but are not part of the stated 116-college count.
