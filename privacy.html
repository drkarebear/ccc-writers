Privacy

Privacy by design.

CCC Writers is designed to collect as little information about visitors as practical. The public site has no analytics, advertising, tracking pixels, user accounts, or site-set cookies.

What this site collects

CCC Writers itself does not ask you to sign in and does not store personal profiles. Ordinary web hosting still involves standard server requests, which can include information such as an IP address, browser type, requested page, and time of access.

The journal map is optional

The journal directory works without the interactive map. The map loads only after you choose Load interactive map. At that point, your browser requests the Leaflet mapping library from unpkg and map tiles from OpenStreetMap. Those providers receive standard web request information needed to deliver the files. OpenStreetMap also requires web map requests to include a valid referrer.

Use the journal directory and optional map.

Community contribution form

The Add or Update buttons can open a Google-hosted contribution form. The form does not require sign-in and is configured not to collect email addresses automatically. Name and email are optional if a contributor chooses to provide them for follow-up. Responses are reviewed before information is added to the public site.

Once you leave CCC Writers for Google Forms, a college, a literary journal, a CSU or UC website, or another external site, that service's own privacy practices apply.

Cookies and analytics

CCC Writers does not intentionally set cookies and does not use Google Analytics, advertising networks, social-media tracking pixels, or behavioral profiling.
