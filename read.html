Read CCC Writing

Browse literary journals connected to California community colleges. These links go to a journal home, archive, or publication page when one has been verified; they do not automatically mean the linked page is the newest issue.

Why this page exists: Finding somewhere to submit matters, but so does reading the work already being published across the system. Reading a journal before submitting is also one of the best ways to understand what it values.
