Transfer Details Worth Knowing Before You Fall in Love With a Program

UC Berkeley

The Creative Writing Minor is all upper division. Berkeley explicitly says community college courses do not satisfy the minor itself.

UC Santa Cruz

The Creative Writing Concentration is selective, and applicants must complete at least one qualifying lower-division creative writing workshop at UCSC before applying.

UCLA

Creative writing workshops are application-only. UCLA advises transfer students who want the minor to start applying early so they have time to complete the sequence.

What Counts as a Pathway Here?

This research pass includes a formal named undergraduate major, concentration/option, minor, specialization/emphasis, or a closely aligned writing major with a substantial creative writing component. A campus is not listed merely because it offers one creative writing course. Programs were checked against official university department, catalog, and transfer-road-map sources on September 6, 2026.

This pass also reviewed the remaining CSU campuses. Cal Poly Pomona, CSU San Marcos, and Stanislaus State all offer meaningful creative writing coursework or writing-rich English/Literature programs, but I did not find a current dedicated undergraduate creative writing pathway that met this directory’s inclusion rule. That distinction keeps “has a creative writing class” from looking the same as “has a formal pathway.” Send a correction or suggest a program.
