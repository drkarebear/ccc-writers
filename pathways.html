The UC Creative Writing Landscape

All nine undergraduate UC campuses have now been checked. The pathways are not interchangeable, so the directory distinguishes a standalone creative writing degree from literary arts, writing studies, concentrations, specializations, and minors.

Most writing-centered

Riverside, San Diego, and Merced

UC Riverside offers the UC system’s standalone Creative Writing B.A. UC San Diego offers a Literary Arts B.A. UC Merced adds a Writing Studies B.A. Creative Writing Emphasis beginning Fall 2026.

Creative writing inside a major

Davis, UCLA, Santa Barbara, and Santa Cruz

These campuses place creative writing inside an English or Literature degree as an emphasis, concentration, or departmental specialization. Santa Cruz’s concentration is selective.

Add it to another degree

Berkeley and Irvine

Both offer Creative Writing minors. Berkeley’s minor is entirely upper division, which means community college coursework cannot satisfy the minor requirements themselves.

The CSU Creative Writing Landscape

All 22 CSU universities in the Fall 2026 system have now been checked. The directory keeps a formal creative-writing credential separate from a campus that offers meaningful workshops or publishing experience without a named creative-writing program.

Writing-centered degree

San Francisco State

SF State offers a standalone B.A. in Creative Writing. Several other CSUs place creative writing inside an English or Humanities degree as an option, concentration, emphasis, or specialization.

Newly clarified for 2026

Bakersfield and Channel Islands

CSU Bakersfield now lists a Creative Writing Specialization in the English B.A. plus a Creative Writing Minor. CSU Channel Islands explicitly maps the English AA-T to its Creative Writing option.

Coursework, not a named credential

SLO, Pomona, San Marcos, and Stanislaus

These campuses offer substantial creative writing or literary-publishing coursework, but current 2026 materials do not identify a separate undergraduate creative-writing credential. Their cards say that plainly.

Transfer Details Worth Knowing Before You Fall in Love With a Program

UC Berkeley

The Creative Writing Minor is all upper division. Berkeley explicitly says community college courses do not satisfy the minor itself.

UC Santa Cruz

The Creative Writing Concentration is selective, and applicants must complete at least one qualifying lower-division creative writing workshop at UCSC before applying.

UCLA

Creative writing workshops are application-only. UCLA advises transfer students who want the minor to start applying early so they have time to complete the sequence.

UC Merced

The new Creative Writing Emphasis begins Fall 2026. Writing Studies also publishes major-specific transfer preparation, including Introduction to Creative Writing as one possible preparation course.

What Counts as a Pathway Here?

The directory includes formal named undergraduate majors, concentrations/options, minors, and specializations/emphases. It also includes a small number of clearly labeled “Coursework Only” routes when a CSU has substantial current creative-writing or literary-publishing coursework but no named 2026 creative-writing credential. All nine undergraduate UCs and all 22 CSU universities were checked against current university, catalog, registrar, or transfer sources on September 7, 2026.

The Fall 2026 CSU audit found four coursework-only routes: Cal Poly San Luis Obispo, Cal Poly Pomona, CSU San Marcos, and Stanislaus State. This keeps “you can seriously study creative writing here” visible without turning it into “this school has a Creative Writing major.” Send a correction or suggest a program.
