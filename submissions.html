Submission Opportunities

New to Literary Submissions?

Previously unpublished

Usually means the piece has not already appeared in a public publication. Always check the journal’s exact rule.

Simultaneous submission

Sending the same piece to more than one journal at the same time. Some journals allow it; some do not.

Rights

Publication agreements explain what permission you give the journal and what rights you keep. Read them before accepting publication.
