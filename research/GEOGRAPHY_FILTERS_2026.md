# Geography Filter Cleanup — September 7, 2026

The project now uses one geographic vocabulary across journals, submissions, pathway programs, the 116-college coverage census, and events.

## Two levels

### Broad region
- Southern California
- Central California
- Northern California
- Statewide/Online

### Local area
- Los Angeles Area
- Orange County
- Inland Empire
- Ventura Area
- San Diego Area
- Imperial Valley
- Desert
- Central Coast
- Central Valley
- Bay Area
- Sacramento Area
- Sierra/Tahoe
- North Coast
- North/Far North
- Statewide/Online

These are student-facing discovery labels, not official administrative regions. The purpose is practical browsing: a student can choose “Southern California” for a wide search or “Los Angeles Area” / “San Diego Area” when proximity matters more.

## Notable normalization decisions

- The old generic `Southern California` journal/college label is now `Los Angeles Area` where the campus is in the LA orbit.
- `San Diego County` becomes the interface label `San Diego Area`.
- `Ventura County` becomes `Ventura Area`.
- Lake Tahoe Community College is `Sierra/Tahoe`.
- Sierra College is `Sacramento Area`.
- Reedley College is `Central Valley`.
- Calbright is `Statewide/Online`.
- Antelope Valley College is included in `Los Angeles Area` because this taxonomy is designed around practical regional discovery rather than county-name purity.
