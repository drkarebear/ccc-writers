# Pass 2 — CSU Creative Writing Pathways (Fall 2026)

Verified: September 7, 2026

## Scope

This pass checked the Fall 2026 California State University landscape university by university. The CSU identifies a transition to 22 universities in Fall 2026 as Cal Poly San Luis Obispo and the former Cal Maritime integrate; the Vallejo location is now Cal Poly Solano Campus / Cal Poly Maritime Academy rather than a separate university for this census.

The directory now represents all 22 CSU universities. Inclusion does **not** imply that every university has a formal creative-writing credential. Four campuses are intentionally labeled **Coursework Only** when current materials show meaningful creative-writing or literary-publishing study without a named undergraduate creative-writing degree, concentration, emphasis, or minor.

## Results

- 22 CSU universities represented
- 18 CSU records with a formal named creative-writing pathway
- 4 CSU coursework-only routes: Cal Poly San Luis Obispo, Cal Poly Pomona, CSU San Marcos, and Stanislaus State
- Combined with the 9 undergraduate UC campuses: 31 campus writing routes statewide, 27 formal pathways + 4 coursework-only routes

## Important corrections and additions

### Cal Poly San Luis Obispo

The prior dataset called this an “Optional Creative Writing Emphasis.” The current 2026–28 English B.A. does **not** name that emphasis. It does, however, offer upper-division Creative Nonfiction, Fiction Writing, and Poetry Writing. The record is therefore retained as **Coursework Only** rather than a formal pathway.

Official sources:
- https://catalog.calpoly.edu/liberal-arts/english/english-ba/
- https://catalog.calpoly.edu/courses/engl/

### CSU Bakersfield

The current English B.A. in Language and Literature lists a **Creative Writing Specialization**. CSUB also lists a **Creative Writing Minor** in the Fall 2026 program inventory. This is stronger than the old dataset, which represented Bakersfield only by the minor.

Official sources:
- https://catalog.csub.edu/academic-degrees-programs/undergraduate-majors/english-ba-language-literature/
- https://catalog.csub.edu/academic-degrees-programs/

### CSU Channel Islands

The Creative Writing route remains inside the English B.A. CSUCI's current Associate Degrees for Transfer table explicitly maps the **English AA-T** to the **BA English, Creative Writing Option**, so the transfer-strength field is upgraded to `published-ccc-pathway`.

Official source:
- https://www.csuci.edu/office-departments/registrar/tmc.html

### Cal Poly Humboldt

The current structure distinguishes the **Writing Minor** from a separate **Literary Publishing Minor**. The old combined “Creative Writing & Publishing Options” wording was revised so the credential structure is not overstated.

Official sources:
- https://www.humboldt.edu/academics/programs/writing-minor
- https://www.humboldt.edu/english

### Cal Poly Pomona

Current 2026–27 English options are Applied Linguistics, English Education, and Literary Studies; no named Creative Writing option was verified. Creative writing remains available through courses and the Writing Studies minor, including creative writing, creative nonfiction, poetry, genre fiction, and journal publishing. Added as **Coursework Only**.

Official sources:
- https://www.cpp.edu/class/english-modern-languages/academics/majors.shtml
- https://www.cpp.edu/academic-programs/academic-advising/tools/sheets-roadmaps/2026-2027/index.shtml

### CSU San Marcos

Current Literature and Writing Studies offerings include Introduction to Creative Writing, Creative Writing Workshop I, and Advanced Creative Writing. A separate named undergraduate Creative Writing credential was not verified in the 2026 audit, so the campus is included as **Coursework Only**.

Official source:
- https://www.csusm.edu/ltwr/

### Stanislaus State

The current English B.A. explicitly includes electives in creative writing and editing/publishing. *Penumbra* remains an active student-run literary and art journal, and students can participate through ENGL 4019, Editing a Literary Magazine. No separate undergraduate Creative Writing credential was verified, so the campus is included as **Coursework Only**.

Official sources:
- https://www.csustan.edu/english/english-major
- https://www.csustan.edu/penumbra
