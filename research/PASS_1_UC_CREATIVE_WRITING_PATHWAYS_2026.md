# UC Creative Writing Pathways: 2026 Verification Pass

Verified September 7, 2026. This pass reviewed all nine undergraduate University of California campuses using official university department, catalog, registrar, and advising sources.

## Inclusion rule

Include a campus when it offers a formal named undergraduate creative writing major, literary/writing major, concentration, emphasis, specialization, or minor. Do not label a campus as a formal pathway merely because it offers one creative writing course.

## UC landscape

| Campus | Formal pathway | Type | Key transfer/detail note |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| UC Berkeley | Creative Writing Minor | Minor | All minor requirements are upper division; Berkeley states community college courses do not satisfy the minor itself. |
| UC Davis | English B.A., Creative Writing Emphasis | Emphasis | Three-course creative-writing emphasis; advanced workshops are application-based. |
| UC Irvine | Creative Writing Minor | Minor | Seven-course minor focused on prose fiction and poetry. |
| UCLA | English B.A., Creative Writing Concentration; Creative Writing Minor | Concentration + minor | Workshops are application-only; the minor requires a writing sample for admission. |
| UC Merced | Writing Studies B.A., Creative Writing Emphasis | Emphasis | New Fall 2026. Writing Studies publishes specific transfer preparation criteria. |
| UC Riverside | B.A. in Creative Writing; Creative Writing Minor | Standalone major + minor | UCR describes the B.A. as the only standalone Creative Writing B.A. in the UC system. |
| UC San Diego | Literary Arts B.A.; Literary Arts Minor | Major + minor | Replaces the older Literature/Writing major; UCSD publishes transfer-specific substitution guidance. |
| UC Santa Barbara | English B.A., Las Maestras Center/Creative Writing Specialization | Departmental specialization | Four-course specialization within English; departmental rather than a separately named UC degree. |
| UC Santa Cruz | Literature B.A., Creative Writing Concentration | Selective concentration | Applicants must complete at least one qualifying lower-division creative writing workshop at UCSC before applying. |

## Important 2026 changes

### UC Merced
UC Merced's registrar lists **Writing Studies B.A. — Creative Writing Emphasis** with a Fall 2026 start term. The Writing Studies major page also publishes transfer admission criteria requiring at least two approved preparation courses (or equivalents) with C- or better. WRI 025, Introduction to Creative Writing, is one of the listed choices.

Official sources:
- https://registrar.ucmerced.edu/sites/g/files/ufvvjh1551/f/page/documents/bannerstudentcodelist_5.pdf
- https://writingstudies.ucmerced.edu/node/401
- https://writingstudies.ucmerced.edu/courses

### UC San Diego
UC San Diego now lists **Literary Arts (B.A.)** among its current undergraduate majors. The Department of Literature states that its prior Literature/Writing major is being discontinued and replaced by Literary Arts.

Official sources:
- https://literature.ucsd.edu/ugrad/majors/index.html
- https://students.ucsd.edu/academics/advising/majors-minors/undergraduate-majors.html
