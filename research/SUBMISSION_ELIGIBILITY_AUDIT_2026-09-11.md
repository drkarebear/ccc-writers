# Submission Eligibility Audit — September 11, 2026

This audit normalizes the 69 journal records for the student-facing submission finder. It uses the per-record official/journal source research already stored in `journals.json`, with targeted re-verification of ambiguous or high-impact cases on September 11, 2026. Unknown eligibility remains explicitly marked as needing verification rather than inferred.

## Tag definitions

- **All community college students**: all community college students may submit, even when the publication also serves a broader national two-year-college audience.
- **Location-based eligibility**: eligibility is explicitly tied to a geographic community.
- **College students and/or alumni**: current college students and/or alumni are eligible.
- **Open to everyone**: the source explicitly supports unrestricted/public/global eligibility.
- **Needs verification**: current eligibility is incomplete or ambiguous.
