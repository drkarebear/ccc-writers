Built for discovery

One place to see the community that already exists.

The goal is not to become another literary magazine. It is to make California’s community-college writing ecosystem visible, searchable, and easier to enter.

Early prototype

This starter intentionally contains a small verified dataset. The next phase is systematic research across all California Community Colleges, followed by a moderated way for colleges to submit corrections and events.

CCC Writers is a working title for an independent resource supporting California community college creative writers.
